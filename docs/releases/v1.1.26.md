## 📖 About this release

v1.1.26 brings two big pieces of work since v1.1.25:

1. **Attendance devices.** Face-recognition attendance terminals (e.g. Hikvision
   DS-K1T671MF) join cameras as a second attendance source. Both feed the same
   attendance engine, so policies, reports and approvals work unchanged.
2. **A full UI/UX redesign of the console.** Every screen now follows one modern
   design system, tables scroll correctly, empty screens explain what to do next,
   and the most-used screens (employees, attendance, approvals, capture review,
   notifications) were rebuilt for clarity.

It also adds the approved Template 1 PDF reports.

---

## 🧩 What's new

### 1. Attendance devices
- Register face/fingerprint attendance terminals as devices, with credentials
  encrypted at rest.
- **Push ingest:** each terminal posts its taps to a generated URL, so branch
  terminals work without VPN, port forwarding or a fixed IP.
- **Collector pull:** where the terminal can't reach Maugood directly, Maugood reads
  the taps back from the MTS collector every 30 seconds. This is switched off unless
  `MAUGOOD_COLLECTOR_URL` is set.
- Device users are synced and mapped to employees. Device taps become attendance
  events next to camera detections.
- Devices list and detail redesigned: live status, branch, people seen and
  unmapped users at a glance.

### 2. Reports
- **Template 1 ("Classic") PDF reports:** company daily, company date range,
  employee date range and employee single day. Fonts are bundled so PDFs render
  offline. Tables use fixed widths so photo columns never overflow A4.
- Reports page redesigned: report types as cards across the top, one range bar
  (Today / This week / Last 3 days / Last 7 days / Custom) with the download
  buttons, then a full-width live preview.

### 3. New design system (all screens)
- New colours, typography (Inter + Plus Jakarta Sans), buttons, cards, tables,
  pills, tabs, forms, drawers and dialogs, with subtle animations and dark mode.
- Shape-matched skeleton loaders on every screen, plus the MaugoodAI boot loader.
- Every screen separates loading, could not load (with Retry), nothing yet (with
  the next action), no search results (with Clear filters), and real zero values.
- One shared standard for every Add / Edit form: numbered sections, required
  marks, errors under the field, switches, option cards and a fixed Save bar.
- Settings has a grouped side menu: Workspace, Access, Delivery, Organisation data,
  Personal.

### 4. People
- Employee profile is now its own page (`/employees/<id>`): header with photo,
  quick facts, and tabs for Details, Attendance, Camera events, Matched clips and
  Team. The selected tab survives a reload.
- Employees grid shows profile-style cards, four per row, with the employee photo,
  status dot, role and a "Needs photos" marker. Select and the ⋮ menu appear on
  hover.
- Employee photos show as avatars in the employees list and grid.
- "ID" is renamed "Employee ID", and "Users" is renamed "AD Users".
- **Profile & Photo** (self-service): profile card, face-recognition readiness
  (which angles are covered and what to add next), an upload dialog, and a photo
  viewer.
- **Photo approvals:** one card per employee with a photo slider, plus a viewer
  with Approve / Reject.

### 5. Attendance
- **Day detail** has a distinct design for every status, each with a plain
  explanation:
  - Present (on time, early out, short hours, overtime)
  - Late (Fixed and Flex)
  - Present via escalation
  - Absent, with a "why this day is absent" checklist, request progress and
    camera-offline notes
  - Not in yet
  - On leave
  - Week off
  - Worked on week off
  - Holiday
  - Worked on holiday
  - No record
  - Future
- **My Attendance:** today card, month summary tiles, last 14 days, mini calendar,
  and Request leave / Submit exception.
- **Calendar** redesign, with company and per-employee views.
- **Manager dashboard:** today's team (Present, Late, Absent, On leave, approvals
  waiting), this month's attendance trend, a "Needs your attention" list, and the
  team roster. My Team opens the full employee profile.

### 6. Camera evidence
- **Camera Logs:** summary tiles, quick date ranges, day grouping, exact times,
  person links, confidence bars, list and grid views, and an event detail panel.
- **Unidentified Faces:**
  - summary tiles and one flat tab bar (Unknown faces, Similarity groups, Mapped)
  - day grouping, select mode with bulk "Map to employee", and a face viewer
  - redesigned similarity-group cards
- **Clip Logs:**
  - Saved clips and Logs only tabs, with summary tiles
  - day grouping with a 24-hour strip, list and grid views
  - clip drawer with the video player
- Person clips can be filtered by match result.

### 7. Shell, navigation and sign-in
- New login page: Omani banner, show/hide password, required-field marks, and
  icon-only Microsoft / Google sign-in.
- User menu shows who is signed in and their role, with Settings and Log out.
  A "Sign out?" confirmation shows the workspace's own (client) logo.
- The tenant's display name appears next to its logo in the sidebar.
- **Notifications:**
  - The bell lists unread notifications only, in compact rows with category icons.
  - The Notifications page has clickable cards, plus Clear / Clear all. These hide
    notifications on the page only; nothing is deleted, and Show cleared brings
    them back.
  - Clearing plays a short animation.

## 🐞 Fixes and refinements

1. **Table headers:** rows no longer show through above the header while
   scrolling, and headers pin directly under the top bar on every screen.
2. **Wide tables:** they scroll inside their own card instead of pushing the page
   sideways.
3. **Daily attendance:** only the table header stays fixed, instead of half the
   screen.
4. **Long values:** names wrap and long emails and file names are shortened, so
   tables fit laptop screens.
5. **Dropdowns:** the notification, language and user menus are no longer hidden
   behind fixed page headers.
6. **Reports:** the Excel button no longer stays on "Downloading…".
7. **Dashboards:** the Employee dashboard "Invalid Date" is fixed, and HR punctuality
   now skips weekend days.
8. **My Attendance:** weekend days are taken from the calendar, so they show as
   Week off.

## 🗄️ Database and backend changes

- **Migrations:**
  - `0094_attendance_devices`
  - `0095_device_users_events`, which adds `detection_events.source` and
    `device_id`, and makes `camera_id` nullable
  - `0096_device_push_tokens`, a global `public.device_push_tokens` registry

  Run `scripts.migrate` on deployment; the entrypoint does this automatically.
- **New endpoint:** `GET /api/employees/{id}/photos/{photo_id}/thumb`, a small
  avatar thumbnail. Employee list rows now carry `primary_photo_id`.
- **New setting:** `MAUGOOD_COLLECTOR_URL`. It is optional, and the collector pull
  stays off when it is unset.

## 🚧 Known issues and deferred

- **Manager rejection notice:** the backend test
  `test_manager_reject_fires_approval_decided_for_employee` fails. When a Manager
  rejects a request, the employee may not get the "decided" notification. This
  existed before this release and needs a fix.
- **Weekend flag:** `/api/attendance/me/recent` marks weekend days as working days
  (`is_weekend=false`), so the Employee dashboard can show Saturday as Absent. My
  Attendance works around it; the API needs a fix.
- **Absent before the shift:** today can show Absent before a late shift has
  started, where "Not in yet" is expected.
- **Shift data:** the shift "Shift 2 16:00–23:59" is configured to end at 02:03, so
  the name or the setting needs correcting. A day with in = out (0 hours) still
  counts as Present.
- **Narrow screens:** at exactly 1280px wide, the Employees and Clip Analytics
  tables scroll sideways inside their card. They fit at 1366px and above.
- **Desktop only:** this release supports desktop web browsers only.
- **Arabic:** new Arabic text is machine-generated and needs the Omran HR
  native-speaker review.
- **Old styling:** three large Clip Analytics dialogs (batch identify, batch
  status, live processing) still use the older styling.
- **Department codes:** Add department does not check the code format as you type.

## 🔒 Privacy and security

- **Encryption:** face photos, crops, embeddings, RTSP addresses, device
  credentials and provider secrets stay encrypted at rest.
- **Secrets:** they are write-only in Settings and are never shown back.
- **Tenant isolation:** unchanged; the isolation test suites pass.
- **Thumbnail audit change:** employee-list photo thumbnails are not written to the
  audit log for each view, so the log doesn't fill on every page of the list.
  Opening a full-size employee photo is still audited.
- **Notifications:** clearing a notification hides it in this browser only; no
  notification record is deleted.
