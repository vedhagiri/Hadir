1Three-state matrix — what does "absent" actually mean?

The current build collapses three very different situations into a single "Absent" label. Operators can't tell why an employee is absent, which makes triage impossible. The proposal differentiates three states at the engine level and surfaces them with distinct visuals.

Real absence

No event captured in the shift window; no approved exception.

  • Cell tint: danger-soft · banner: danger
  • Detection events present: 0
  • Operator action: confirm absence, optionally request comment from employee
  • Counts toward "Absent" KPI

Camera miss New

Detection events exist but none matched the employee, OR positioning was off.

  • Cell tint: miss-soft · banner: miss (teal)
  • Detection events present: ≥ 1, none matched
  • Operator action: review camera evidence, raise escalation if needed
  • NOT counted toward "Absent" KPI — surfaced separately on dashboards

Exception approved

Employee submitted an exception; Manager + HR approved.

  • Cell tint: success-soft · banner: success
  • Approval chain visible in drawer
  • Operator action: none — drawer shows the audit trail
  • Counts as "Excused" on KPIs, not absent
Engineering note: This split is the "waiting status taxonomy" work in MAU-021 + MAU-022. The status enum gains camera_miss and excused server-side. Single source of truth in attendance_calendar/queries.py; consumed identically by Calendar, Daily Attendance, Employee tab, Reports.

12Status legend — final taxonomy

Every status from MAU-022's canonical enum, with the exact label, dot colour, and one-line meaning. Used by Calendar, Daily Attendance, Employee tab, and Reports identically.

Present
Captured + total ≥ required hours.
Late
Captured but in-time past grace minutes.
Real absence
No event captured, no approved exception.
Camera miss New
Events captured but employee not matched.
Excused New
Approved exception covers the day.
Waiting
Today only — within open shift window.
Leave / Holiday
Approved leave or tenant holiday covers the day.
Weekend / Future / No record
Days not expected to have captures.
