Anomaly Notes — Design Proposal

Contextual intelligent notes shown near image preview sections across Camera Logs, Daily Attendance, Clip Analytics, Calendar Evidence, and Employee tabs. Purpose: help users understand why events or images are missing or unclear, and surface attendance anomalies during verification.

1 — Note component variants

Two display formats: inline pill (used inside table rows and evidence tiles) and block note (used below a group of crops or in attendance cards).

Inline pills

⚠ Low match confidence ⚠ Face not captured ℹ Face partially captured ℹ Unmatched — not in database ⚠ Former employee detected ⚠ Camera offline period ⚠ Low detection quality ℹ Very small face crop ℹ No crop on disk

Block notes (below a group)

⚠Camera positioning issue detected — several events show partial face crops. Consider adjusting camera angle or mounting height for better coverage.
⚠Camera was offline for 47 min during the shift window — attendance events may be incomplete. Raise an escalation request if attendance was affected.
ℹFace detected but could not be matched to any employee. The person may not be enrolled or may be a visitor.
⚠Attendance anomaly — employee was marked Late in and Early out. Review detection events below to verify the recorded times.
2 — Decision logic (client-side, no new API)
Condition (data already available) Note shown Tone Where
has_crop === false Face not captured — no crop image available danger Camera Logs, Emp Events
confidence < 0.30 Very low match confidence — face may be partially blocked danger Camera Logs, Emp Events, Calendar Evidence
confidence >= 0.30 && < 0.50 Low match confidence — identity may be uncertain warning Camera Logs, Emp Events, Calendar Evidence
confidence === null && has_crop Face detected but not matched to any employee info Camera Logs, Emp Events
former_employee_match === true Former employee detected warning Camera Logs, Emp Events
quality_score < 0.30 (FaceCropOut) Low face quality — possible blur or partial capture warning Clip Analytics
detection_score < 0.50 (FaceCropOut) Face not detected clearly — low detection score info Clip Analytics
width < 60 || height < 60 (FaceCropOut) Face partially captured — very small crop area info Clip Analytics
match_confidence === null (FaceCropOut) No match confidence — face unmatched or legacy record neutral Clip Analytics
camera_gaps.length > 0 (DayDetail) Camera was offline N min during shift — events may be missing danger Calendar Evidence, Emp Attendance
evidence.length === 0 && status === "present" Attendance recorded but no face evidence crops available warning Calendar Evidence
late || early_out || short_hours Attendance anomaly — [late / early out / short hours] detected warning Daily Attendance, Emp Attendance
absent === true No attendance recorded for this working day danger Daily Attendance, Emp Attendance

4 — Implementation scope

Files to change

  • features/employees/EmployeeViewDrawer.tsx — Camera Events + Attendance tabs

No backend changes required

All note conditions are derived from data already returned by existing API responses. No new endpoints, migrations, or ML models are needed. Notes are computed client-side via a pure helper function.


i18n

All note strings added to en.json and ar.json under a new anomaly.* key block.
